About Me

Automotive photographer, Greenville SC area

I'm Noah, a 14-year-old automotive photographer based in the Greenville, South Carolina area. I've always been interested in cars and photography, so combining the two felt natural.

I enjoy capturing everything from full-car shots to close-up details and action photos at meets and events. My goal is to create clean, high-quality images that show off what makes each car unique.

I'm continuing to improve with every shoot, learn new techniques, and build my portfolio while working with local car owners. Whether it's a private shoot, a car show, or a quick session at a meet, I want the experience to be easy, fun, and worth remembering.
